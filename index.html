About QuickResume

QuickResume is a free, no‑login resume builder. It runs entirely in the browser, storing data locally so nothing ever leaves your device.

Features include 12 modern templates, live side‑by‑side preview, customizable fonts, colours, spacing, and PDF export via the browser’s print engine.
